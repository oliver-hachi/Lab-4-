## 3. Main Themes Extracted Using Google AI Studio

The following themes were generated using Google AI Studio based on simulated interview responses. They are preliminary findings, not validated stakeholder feedback.

### Theme 1: Information Accuracy and Listing Management

**Explanation:** The simulated responses suggest that property professionals and local sponsors may need ways to keep information accurate, update details, and review information before publishing.

**Supporting Responses:**
- Property professional: Keeping property details updated and accurate.
- Property professional: Reviewing details and updating them when something changes.
- Local sponsor: Checking business information before publication.

**Possible Product Requirement:** Allow listing owners to review, edit, and update their information before and after publication.

### Theme 2: Essential Listing Details

**Explanation:** The simulated responses suggest that customers may want important information such as price, location, and property details.

**Supporting Response:**
- Property professional: Customers may ask about price, location, and property details.

**Possible Product Requirement:** Display important property details in a clear and consistent format.

### Theme 3: Business Discovery and Success Measures

**Explanation:** The simulated responses suggest that local sponsors may value a platform that helps customers discover their businesses.

**Supporting Responses:**
- Local sponsor: Customers may find businesses online or through recommendations.
- Local sponsor: More people discovering the business could show that the platform is helpful.

**Possible Product Requirement:** Consider tracking basic discovery metrics, such as listing views or clicks on contact links.

## 4. Working Hypothesis Persona: Property Professional

**Goals:**
- Keep property details accurate.
- Update listings easily.
- Check information before publishing.

**Challenges:**
- Keeping information updated.
- Managing property details.

**Possible Needs:**
- An easy way to manage listings.
- A way to review listings before publishing.
- A clear format for displaying important property details.

## 5. Working Hypothesis Persona: Local Sponsor

**Goals:**
- Promote a local business.
- Keep business information accurate.
- Help more customers discover the business.

**Challenges:**
- Updating business information.
- Reaching local customers.
- Understanding whether the platform helps people discover the business.

**Possible Needs:**
- An easy way to manage business information.
- A way to review information before publishing.
- A way to track customer interest.

## 6. Limitations

These themes and personas are based on simulated responses and AI-assisted analysis. They are not confirmed stakeholder needs. Real stakeholder interviews would be needed to validate the findings.
