# Lab 4 Step 4: Interview Notes and Personas

## Interview Status

These are simulated interviews because we do not have an approved real participant. The answers are examples, not real interview results.

## 1. Simulated Interview: Property Professional

**Question:** How do you manage property listings?

**Simulated Response:** I need to keep property details updated and accurate.

**Question:** How do you check that property information is correct?

**Simulated Response:** I review the details and update them when something changes.

**Question:** What details do customers ask about most?

**Simulated Response:** Customers may ask about price, location, and property details.

## 2. Simulated Interview: Local Sponsor

**Question:** How do customers usually find your business?

**Simulated Response:** Customers may find us online or through recommendations.

**Question:** How should business information be reviewed before publishing?

**Simulated Response:** Business owners should be able to check their information before it is published.

**Question:** How would you know if the platform is useful?

**Simulated Response:** More people discovering the business could show that the platform is helpful.

## 3. Main Themes

These themes are based on simulated responses and need to be checked with real stakeholders later.

- Keeping information accurate and updated.
- Making information easy to manage.
- Reviewing information before publishing.
- Helping people find properties and local businesses.

## 4. Working Hypothesis Persona: Property Professional

**Goals:**
- Keep property details accurate.
- Update listings easily.
- Check information before publishing.

**Challenges:**
- Keeping information updated.
- Managing property details.

**Possible Needs:**
- An easy way to manage listings.
- A way to review listings before publishing.

## 5. Working Hypothesis Persona: Local Sponsor

**Goals:**
- Promote a local business.
- Keep business information accurate.
- Help more customers discover the business.

**Challenges:**
- Updating business information.
- Reaching local customers.

**Possible Needs:**
- An easy way to manage business information.
- A way to review information before publishing.
- A way to track customer interest.
