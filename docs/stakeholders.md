# Lab 4 STEP 1
## Stakeholders and Assumptions

## Stakeholder Groups
1. Property Professional: Someone who manages property listings.
2. Local Sponsor: A local business featured on the platform.

## Known Facts
- Our project is a neighborhood listing platform.
- The platform includes property and sponsor information.

## Assumptions
- Property professionals want an easy way to manage listings.
- Property professionals want accurate property information.
- Local sponsors want to promote their businesses.
- Local sponsors want their information to be accurate.
- Both groups may want to approve information before it is published.
